KLAYWORLD

A small walkable environment study inspired by the tactile, handmade world-building of classic clay animation games.

Controls
WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to hop, Esc to pause. Click below to enter pointer-lock first-person mode.

Three.js is bundled locally, no internet needed. Best on desktop with a mouse.
